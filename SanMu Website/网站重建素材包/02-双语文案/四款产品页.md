# 四款产品页双语文案

## SanMu EQ6
中文短句：把频率判断留在眼前，把决定留给耳朵。
中文正文：SanMu EQ6 是一款最多 24 段的动态参量均衡器。频谱、频点卡片、Pre/Post 和 Mid/Side 放置都围绕同一件事：更快找到需要处理的地方，同时保留真实的听感判断。
中文特性：频点工作区（拖动或输入 Hz/kHz/dB/Q，Solo、斜率、通道放置）；动态均衡（每段独立范围、阈值和 Auto Threshold）；两种处理模式（Zero Latency / Linear Phase）。
English short line: Keep the frequency decision visible. Keep the final call in your ears.
English body: SanMu EQ6 is a dynamic parametric EQ with up to 24 band slots. Spectrum, band cards, Pre/Post and Mid/Side placement all serve one goal: get to the right move faster while keeping listening at the centre.
English features: Band workspace; Dynamic EQ; Zero Latency and Linear Phase.

## SanMu Comp
中文短句：四种压缩手感，一套清楚的动态工作区。
中文正文：SanMu Comp 把 LEV、VCA、FET、Opto 放在同一套可比较的界面里。每种模式保持独立参数与独立动态行为，帮助你从素材出发选择合适的反应速度和密度。
中文补充：LISTEN 当前公开流程使用 5 秒素材与手动 0.5–24 dB 目标测算；结果仅供参考。预览没有宿主音频时不会伪造结果。
English short line: Four compression behaviours. One readable dynamics workspace.
English body: SanMu Comp brings LEV, VCA, FET and Opto into one comparable interface. Each mode keeps its own controls and dynamic behaviour.
English note: LISTEN measures a manual 0.5–24 dB target from a 5-second clip; it is a reference, not a replacement for listening.

## SanMu Delay
中文短句：先决定时间，再决定回声如何离开。
中文正文：SanMu Delay 用一个主时间编辑器连接 Modern、Tape、BBD、Diffused 四种引擎，并提供 Independent、Ping-Pong、Cross 三种路由。L/R 节点、LINK 和 SYNC/BPM/FREE 都在同一处完成。
中文补充：BPM 默认 120；SYNC 跟随宿主；FREEZE 循环已有回声但不是无限衰减。
English short line: Set the time first. Then decide where the echo goes.
English body: SanMu Delay connects four engines to one main time editor, with Independent, Ping-Pong and Cross routing. L/R nodes, LINK and SYNC/BPM/FREE all live in the same workspace.
English note: BPM defaults to 120; SYNC follows the host; FREEZE recirculates existing echoes but is not infinite decay.

## SanMu Reverb
中文短句：把空间拆开看，再把它合回音乐。
中文正文：SanMu Reverb 以 Plate、Room、Hall、Chamber 四类空间为入口，将 Predelay、Decay、阻尼、Size、早晚扩散、调制和 EQ 放进同一套声场工作区。干、湿、混合视图让空间变化有清晰的视觉对应。
中文补充：旋钮分区为 Time/Master、Damping、Shape、Diff、Mod、EQ；声场图和水波背景是混响专属工作区。
English short line: See the space in parts. Bring it back into the music.
English body: SanMu Reverb starts with Plate, Room, Hall and Chamber, then places Predelay, Decay, damping, Size, early/late diffusion, modulation and EQ in one spatial workspace.
English note: The sound-field and ripple visuals belong to Reverb's workspace.
