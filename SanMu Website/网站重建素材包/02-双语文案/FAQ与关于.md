# FAQ、关于与支持页

## FAQ 中文

SanMu Audio 是围绕频率、动态、时间和空间制作的插件系列。四款插件可单独使用，也可以组合成一条工作链。当前工程目标包含 VST3、AU、AAX，最终支持范围以经过签名、安装验证并公开的构建为准。

当前安装包尚未开放，价格暂不展示。网站先用于介绍产品方向、真实音乐响应截图和功能边界。EQ6 支持最多 24 个频段槽位，Linear Phase 位于设置中；Comp 包含 LEV、VCA、FET、Opto 四种独立压缩行为，LISTEN 为手动目标的五秒测算；Delay 使用四种引擎、三种路由和 Echo time editor；Reverb 提供 Hall、Plate、Room、Chamber 四类空间。

反馈请发送至 contact@sanmuaudio.com，并附上产品、版本、系统、宿主、复现步骤以及截图或音频片段。

## FAQ English

SanMu Audio is a plug-in series for frequency, dynamics, time and space. Each plug-in works on its own or in a chain. The current projects target VST3, AU and AAX; final coverage will be listed against signed, tested public builds.

Installers are not open yet and prices are intentionally not shown. The site currently introduces the product direction, real playback captures and feature boundaries. EQ6 supports up to 24 band slots with Linear Phase in Settings; Comp contains four independent behaviours—LEV, VCA, FET and Opto—with a five-second LISTEN measurement; Delay connects four engines and three routings to one Echo time editor; Reverb provides Hall, Plate, Room and Chamber spaces.

Send feedback to contact@sanmuaudio.com with the product, version, OS, host, reproduction steps and any useful screenshots or audio.

## 关于 / About

中文标题：为声音工作者制作。

中文正文：我们把每一次界面决策都放回真实的混音动作里：找到问题、做出调整、比较前后，然后继续工作。网站使用真实音乐播放中的插件界面捕捉，清理宿主外框与无关提示，保留响应层和读数。当前网站先整理产品方向；安装包、授权方案与正式下载地址确认后，再开放入口。

English title: Built for sound workers.

English body: We put every interface decision back into a real mixing move: find the issue, make the change, compare before and after, then keep working. The site uses plug-in captures made during real playback, cleaning host chrome and unrelated prompts while keeping the response layer and readouts. Installers, licensing and public download addresses will open after they are confirmed.
